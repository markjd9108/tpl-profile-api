Sample library

Sample participant profiles and sample leader reports

Every participant receives a private profile on the day of the session. After a Focused Session it covers one skill: their result, what they do well, their growth step, how they naturally work, how they felt through the session, and three practical next moves. After a Workshop it covers all three skills and names the person’s archetype, the pattern their three results form together.

Each profile below is a real hosted page, built by the same system that builds a participant's own profile. Open one on a phone or tablet and it behaves exactly as a participant would see it. The people are invented and the employer is Sample Company Ltd.

After a Workshop, leaders receive the Leadership Insight Report: team-level patterns, strengths and gaps, with clear actions. After a Focused Session, they receive the Team Performance Snapshot: a short read on the team's level in the skill, one strength, one gap with an action, and the next step. Neither report shows an individual's profile.

One skill per profile

Each skill is assessed through three hands-on simulations and its own framework. A workshop that covers more than one skill gives each person a result for every skill they worked on.

How results are built

After each simulation, people answer short situational questions about their own approach and their teammates'. The two views are blended into one result.

Privacy

Profiles are private to the participant. These sample pages are unlisted and kept out of search, so they are safe to share and to open in front of visitors.
