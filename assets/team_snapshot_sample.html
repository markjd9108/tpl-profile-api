GAP · CLOSE FIRST

Agreeing the result before the work starts

9OF 14

started work without agreeing what the finished result should look like.

SIMULATION · AMBIGUITY & ASSUMPTION
AT WORKPeople follow the instructions correctly but work toward different results. Problems appear at the end of the task, when fixing them costs the most.
WHAT TO DOAgree the finished result before starting. This week's action is below.
YOUR ACTION THIS WEEK

Agree the finished result before any task starts.

Before work begins, the person giving the task says what done looks like and when it is due. The person receiving it repeats it back in their own words. These are the first two steps of The Clarity Loop: Clarify and Check.

EACH PERSON'S IF-THEN PLANWhen I take on a task, I agree what done looks like and by when before I start. ASK IN ONE-TO-ONESOn your last task, what did you and the other person agree the finished result would be?
