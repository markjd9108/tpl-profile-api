02 · Strength and gap

What to keep, and what to fix first

Checking before signing off
10of 14

checked the other person understood before they signed the work off.

One Way

Errors are caught at the end of a task. Keep it: thank people when they ask for the work to be described back.

Starting before the result is agreed
9of 14

started work without agreeing what the finished result should look like.

Ambiguity & Assumption

At work this shows up as tasks done to the wrong standard and redone, even though everyone followed the instructions.

Your action this week
Close every handover with the expected result.

End each handover with two sentences: what the result should look like, and by when. The receiver says it back in their own words. The Clarity Loop: Clarify, then Check.

Each person's if-then plan"When I hand over work, I end with what done looks like and by when."
